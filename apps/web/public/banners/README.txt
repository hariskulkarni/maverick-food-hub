Storefront hero banners (full-bleed carousel images).

Drop banner images here and reference them from
src/lib/storefront-banners.ts (slug -> ['/banners/<file>', ...]).

Recommended: 2:1 ratio (e.g. 2600x1300), JPG/WebP, < 500KB each.

Bowl & Barbeque storefront (/r/bowl-and-barbeque) expects:
  - bowl-and-barbeque-1.jpg   (FRESH FROM THE PAN slide)
  - bowl-and-barbeque-2.jpg   (NO THIRD PARTIES slide)
Until these files exist, the carousel shows a graceful gradient placeholder.
