## What it is

If something went wrong with your order, tell us straight away with your order number. For orders that were materially incorrect, missing items or never delivered, we will arrange a refund or credit as set out in our [Refund & Cancellation Policy](/refunds).

## How to report it

1. Go to [Help with an order](/help). You need to be signed in.
2. Under **What went wrong?** choose the closest option: Order is late, Wrong item, Missing item, Food quality, Order not received, Payment problem, Refund request, Delivery partner issue, or Something else.
3. Under **Describe the problem**, explain what happened.
4. Add your **Order ID** if it isn't already filled in.
5. Tap **Submit report**. Our support team will follow up.

For urgent help with a live order, use the **Support** or **WhatsApp** buttons on your order page, or the phone and WhatsApp details on the [contact page](/contact).

## Use the contact form

You can also write to us from the form on the [contact page](/contact) – no sign-in needed:

1. Enter **Your name** and **Email**.
2. Pick a **Topic**: Help with an order, Refund or payment, My account, Restaurant partnership, Riding with us, or Something else.
3. Write your **Message** (include your order code if it's about an order) and tap **Send message**.

Your message opens a support ticket with our team, and you'll see "Message sent. Our support team will reply by email." Replies come to the email address you entered.

## Tips for a faster resolution

- Report as soon as possible — ideally within 24 hours of delivery.
- Be specific: which items were wrong or missing, and what you received instead.
- Keep photos of incorrect or damaged items; support may ask for them.

## Good to know

- We review each report, with the restaurant where needed, and confirm the outcome with you.
- A refund may not apply if the order couldn't be delivered because the address was wrong or you were unreachable, or for taste preferences where the item matches its description.
- See [Refunds](help:customer-refunds) for where refunded money goes.
