## What you can pay with

The **Payment** section at checkout shows the options available for that restaurant:

- **Pay online** — through the restaurant's payment partner. Depending on the restaurant this is labelled "Pay online (UPI / Card / Wallet)", secured by Razorpay, or "Pay online (UPI / Card / Netbanking)", secured by PhonePe. You are taken to the partner's payment page to complete it.
- **UPI (Flavrly PG — experimental)** — offered only at some restaurants. You pay by UPI directly, and the order is confirmed after your bank verifies the payment.
- **Cash on Delivery** — pay the rider when the order arrives.

## What you pay

The **Order summary** at checkout always shows the subtotal, any discount, wallet or loyalty amount used, tax, restaurant packaging and the delivery fee, followed by the **Total**. The same breakdown is kept on your order page.

## Is my payment information safe?

Yes. Online card and netbanking payments are handled by our payment partners; we never see or store your full card details — only a transaction reference and status.

For direct UPI payments:

- We confirm your payment by matching the bank's credit to your order (amount, bank reference/UTR and time). Screenshots are not accepted as proof of payment.
- Payments flagged by our risk checks may be held for review before the order is confirmed.
- We never ask for your UPI PIN and never send UPI "collect" requests. Never share your UPI PIN or OTP with anyone.

## Good to know

- If a cash-on-delivery payment fails, that option may be disabled for your account (see our [Terms](/terms)).
- For a UPI problem such as money debited but not received, contact support — see [Report a problem with an order](help:customer-report-a-problem).
