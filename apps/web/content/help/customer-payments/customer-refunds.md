## When you may get a refund

Refunds can be issued for orders that were delivered with a problem, could not be delivered, or were cancelled. Typical cases include:

- The order was never delivered.
- The restaurant cancelled the order after you paid.
- Items were missing, wrong or materially different from what you ordered.
- The food arrived spilled, spoiled or clearly unsafe.
- You were charged incorrectly.

Refunds can be full or partial. To ask for one, [report a problem with your order](help:customer-report-a-problem).

## Where the money goes

Approved refunds are sent to one of two places:

- **Your wallet** — this is the usual option. The amount is credited to your wallet straight away, appears on your **Profile**, and can be used at checkout on your next order. Cash-on-delivery orders have no online payment to reverse, so they are refunded this way.
- **Your original payment method** — support can instead reverse the payment through the payment partner you paid with. The time it takes to reach your account depends on your bank or payment provider.

## Good to know

- A refund can never be more than what you paid for the order, even across several partial refunds.
- Where a coupon was used, the refund is based on the amount you actually paid.
- Promotional credits used on a cancelled order come back as credit, not cash.
- For a UPI problem such as money debited but not received, you can also raise a dispute through your UPI app or bank.
- Full details are in our [Refund & Cancellation Policy](/refunds).
