## Coupons and discounts

1. At checkout, type the code into the **Coupon** box and tap **Apply**.
2. If the code works for your order, you will see "Discount applied — you saved ₹…" and the **Discount** line appears in the **Order summary**.
3. If it doesn't, you will see that the code doesn't apply to this order.

The discount is applied to your total before payment. Coupons belong to a restaurant and have their own terms — for example a minimum order value, a maximum discount, validity dates or a limit on how many times they can be used.

## Wallet

Your wallet balance is shown on your **Profile**. Refunds are often credited to your wallet — see [Refunds](help:customer-refunds).

To use it, switch on **Wallet** under **Use rewards** at checkout. Checkout applies up to ₹500 of your wallet balance to an order, shown as the **Wallet** line in the summary.

## Loyalty points

Your loyalty points are also shown on your **Profile**. Switch on **Loyalty** under **Use rewards** at checkout to use them: 1 point = ₹1, and checkout applies up to 200 points per order.

## Good to know

- If a refund is due on an order where you used a coupon, it is calculated on the amount you actually paid.
- Promotional credits used on a cancelled order are returned as credit, not cash (see our [Refund & Cancellation Policy](/refunds)).
- Some restaurants add a free item once your cart reaches a set amount — checkout tells you when you have earned it.
