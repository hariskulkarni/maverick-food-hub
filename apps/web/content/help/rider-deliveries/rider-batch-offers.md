## What it is

When there are more ready orders than free riders, Flavrly may offer an extra order to riders who are already out on a delivery and whose route passes near the new pickup. The offer appears as a full-screen **Quick batch offer**.

You only get batch offers while you're online.

## What the offer shows

- **Add this delivery?** and the restaurant branch to pick up from
- A countdown ring in **seconds**. It turns red in the last 5 seconds.
- **EXTRA EARNINGS**: what you'll earn for the added order
- The extra **detour** in km and the minutes **to pickup**

## How to respond

- Tap **Accept** to add the order to your trip.
- Tap **Decline** if you don't want it.

If you don't respond before the timer runs out, the offer is declined automatically and disappears.

## Good to know

- The same order can be offered to more than one rider. The first rider to accept gets it, and the offer disappears for everyone else. If the order was already taken, accepting won't work.
- An accepted batch order becomes one of your active deliveries. Each one has its own steps and its own delivery code. See [Complete a delivery step by step](help:rider-delivery-steps).
- The Home tab's **CURRENT DELIVERY** card shows one delivery at a time. Finish it to see the next one.
