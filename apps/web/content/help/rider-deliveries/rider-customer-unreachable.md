## Before you report it

Most "unreachable" customers are just hard to find. At the drop location:

1. Re-read the **DROP** details (address, floor/flat, landmark, note) and the **CUSTOMER NOTE**.
2. Tap the phone icon next to the customer's details and let it ring fully.
3. Wait a few minutes and call again. Ask a guard or neighbour politely if there is one.

## Report the customer unreachable

The **Customer unreachable** button appears under the delivery code box once you have tapped **I've reached the customer**.

1. Tap **Customer unreachable**. The app asks you to call the customer first: tap **Call customer** to try again, or **I've already called**.
2. Add a short note for support if you like, such as how many times you called or that the gate is locked. The note is optional.
3. Tap **Alert support**, then confirm.

The screen then shows **Customer unreachable** with *Flavrly support has been alerted; wait for instructions*, and Home shows the order as **Customer unreachable**.

## While you wait

- Stay at the drop location with the order and keep the bag closed.
- Flavrly support will contact you and tell you whether to wait longer, leave the order somewhere or take it elsewhere. Tap **Check for updates** to refresh the screen; it also refreshes on its own every 30 seconds.
- If the customer turns up, ask for their delivery code and tap **Confirm delivery** as usual.
- If support closes the order, the screen shows **Closed by Flavrly support**. Follow the instructions support gave you for the food, then tap **Back to home**.

## If something goes wrong

- **"You're offline — support was NOT alerted"**: your note is kept. Tap **Alert support** again when you have signal, or call rider support.
- **"Tap "I've reached the customer" first…"**: go back, tap **I've reached the customer**, then report again.
- You can also message support from **Profile > Messages** or raise an **Order issue** ticket in **Help & Support** at any time.

## Never

- Never type a guessed code or tap **Confirm delivery** for an order you have not handed over.
- Never leave food at a door or with a guard unless the customer or Flavrly support has told you to.
- Never keep, eat or give away the food.
