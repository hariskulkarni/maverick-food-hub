## Only while you're on duty

Flavrly records the items below **only while you are online or on an active delivery**. When you go offline or sign out, nothing more is sent.

## What is recorded

- **Location**: your GPS position about every 30 seconds while you wait for orders, and about every 15 seconds or 30 metres on a delivery. This builds the route of each delivery.
- **Online and offline times**: when you go online or offline, when the app stops responding and you are set offline automatically, and when your signal is lost and comes back during a delivery.
- **Device and network info**: your phone model, Android or iOS version, Flavrly app version, network type (Wi-Fi or mobile data, plus 4G or 5G when your phone reports it), battery level and whether the phone is charging.

## What is never collected

- Your IMEI or other phone identifiers.
- The list of other apps on your phone, or which apps you are using.
- Your calls, call history or whether you are on a call.

## Why

- So customers, restaurants and Flavrly support can follow a delivery.
- To check a delivery's distance and time if there is a question about it.
- To fix problems: for example, when your location stops updating, support can see whether the phone lost signal, the battery was low, or the app needs an update.

## Who can see it

Only the Flavrly platform team, on your rider page. Restaurants and customers see your live position only during their own delivery, as before.

## How long it is kept

- Route points (GPS history): 90 days.
- Online/offline history: 90 days.
- Device, network and battery history: 30 days.

After that it is deleted automatically. A short summary of each delivery (distance and time, no map points) is kept with the order.

## Good to know

- Device info is sent by the latest version of the rider app. Update the app from the store to get it.
- To stop all of this, go offline on **Home** or sign out.
