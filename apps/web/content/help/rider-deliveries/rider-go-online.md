## What it is

You only receive new-order alerts and can only claim orders while you're **online**. The switch is on the **Home** tab.

## How to go online

1. Open the **Home** tab.
2. Flip the switch on the status card. It changes to **You're online**, with *Receiving delivery requests* underneath.
3. To stop, flip it back. It shows **You're offline**.

If the change doesn't go through, you'll see **Could not update status** and the switch goes back to how it was. Check your connection and try again.

## While you're online

- **New-order popups**: when a restaurant marks a new order ready, a **New order ready** popup appears on any tab. See [Claim orders from the pool](help:rider-claim-orders-from-pool).
- **Location**: the app shares your GPS position the whole time you're online. Allow location access when your phone asks.
- **Sync**: the app checks in with Flavrly about every 30 seconds. Under the switch, **Synced … ago** shows when it last checked in.

> If the app stops checking in for about 2 minutes (for example, your phone sleeps, the app is closed, or you lose network), you can be switched offline automatically. When you come back, check that the switch still says **You're online**.

## Take a break

The **Take a break** card on Home lets you mark a short break:

1. Tap **Take a break**.
2. Choose **15 min**, **30 min** or **60 min**.
3. The card shows **On a break**, *Until HH:MM · N min left*. Tap **End break now** to finish early.

A break does not switch you offline. New-order popups keep coming while the switch is on, so go offline if you don't want any orders.

## Good to know

- Booking or starting a shift doesn't switch you online. Use the switch on Home as well. See [Book and manage your shifts](help:rider-book-shifts).
- If you try to claim an order while offline, the app tells you to go online first.
- If Flavrly requires safety training before going online and you haven't passed it yet, going online is refused with **Training required**. Tap **Open training** and finish the module first. See [Rider training and certificates](help:rider-training).
