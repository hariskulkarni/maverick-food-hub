## What it is

After a delivery, customers can rate it. You can see your own delivery ratings in the app.

## Open Customer Feedback

Go to **Profile > Customer Feedback**. You'll see:

- At the top: your average delivery rating, *across N reviews*.
- One row per review, with the star rating (or *No delivery rating*), the customer's comment if there is one, and the date.

If no one has rated you yet, it says **No customer feedback yet**.

## What you can and can't see

You only see the **delivery** rating, never the customer's rating of the food. A customer's comment appears only if they chose to share it with the rider.

## How your rating is calculated

Your rating, shown on **Earnings** and **My Tier**, is the average of the delivery ratings from your most recent 100 rated deliveries. Older ratings drop out as new ones come in.

- New riders start at 5.0.
- Reviews hidden or flagged by moderation don't count.

## Why it matters

Your rating, together with your total deliveries, decides your tier. See [Incentives, surge pay and your tier](help:rider-incentives-surge-tier).
