## What it is

When a restaurant marks an order ready, it goes into the **order pool** on the **Orders** tab. Any eligible rider can claim it. Whoever claims it first gets it.

- **Fleet riders** see ready orders from restaurants across the platform.
- **Dedicated riders** see only orders from their own restaurant (or its group).

## What each order card shows

- Restaurant name, branch, order code and the **Collect from** address
- **you earn**: what you'll be paid for the run
- The items, the distance in km, the order total, and the drop address

## How to claim

1. Make sure you're online. If you're offline, a banner tells you to go online on the Home tab.
2. On **Orders**, tap **Claim this order**.
3. If you get it, you'll see **Order claimed**. Tap **View on Home** to open it as your current delivery.

If another rider got there first, you'll see **Just missed it** and the card disappears.

## The New order ready popup

While you're online, a **New order ready** popup appears on any tab when a new order lands in the pool. It shows the pickup, the drop area, **YOU EARN** and the pickup distance, and your phone sounds and vibrates.

- Tap **VIEW ORDER** to go to the Orders tab and claim it there.
- Tap **DISMISS**, or tap outside the popup, to close it. It closes by itself after 20 seconds.

The popup doesn't claim the order for you. You still need to tap **Claim this order**.

## Good to know

- The amount on the pool card is the standard rate. Your own amount is fixed the moment you claim, and it's shown on your delivery screen and Home card.
- A claimed order starts as accepted. Next, head to the restaurant. See [Complete a delivery step by step](help:rider-delivery-steps).
- You can only claim orders after your rider profile is approved.
- Pull down to refresh the list.
