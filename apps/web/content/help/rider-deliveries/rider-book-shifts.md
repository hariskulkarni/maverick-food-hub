## What it is

**My Shifts** is where you plan when you'll work. You book slots in advance and mark them started and completed as you go.

Open it from **Home > Shifts** (under Quick actions) or **Profile > My Shifts**.

## Book a slot

1. Scroll to **Book a slot**.
2. Under **Day**, pick a day. You can choose today or any of the next 6 days.
3. Pick a **Start time** and an **End time**. Times are offered in 2-hour steps from 08:00 to 22:00, and the end must be after the start.
4. Optionally, type a **Preferred zone** (for example, an area name).
5. Tap **Book this slot**. You'll see **Slot booked**.

The shift appears under **Upcoming shifts** with the status **Booked**.

## Work your shift

- **Booked**: tap **Start shift** when you begin, or **Cancel** to remove it. A cancelled booked shift is deleted from your list.
- **In progress**: tap **Complete shift** when you finish.
- **Completed**, **Missed** and **Cancelled** shifts have no actions.

## Missed shifts

If you don't start a booked shift within 30 minutes of its start time, it can be marked **Missed**. Start it on time, or cancel it beforehand if you can't make it.

## Good to know

- Starting a shift doesn't switch you online, and going online doesn't start a shift. Use the switch on **Home** to receive orders. See [Go online, go offline and take a break](help:rider-go-online).
- Shift times are local Indian time.
- My Shifts lists shifts from today onwards. Pull down to refresh.
