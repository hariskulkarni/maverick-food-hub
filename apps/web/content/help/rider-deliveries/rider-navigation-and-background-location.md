## Open the location in Google Maps

- **Orders** tab: each order card has **Navigate to pickup in Google Maps**, so you can check the route before you claim.
- Delivery screen: the button shows **Navigate to pickup in Google Maps** until you tap **I've picked up the order**, then **Navigate to customer in Google Maps**. It also appears on a **New request** before you accept, so you can preview the pickup.

The button opens driving directions in the Google Maps app on your phone. If Google Maps isn't installed, Android opens another maps app and iPhone opens Apple Maps; as a last resort the directions open in your browser. When the address has no map pin, the address text is searched instead.

## Tracking keeps running while you use Google Maps

Once you accept a delivery (or claim one from the pool), Flavrly keeps sharing your location **in the background** until the delivery ends, even while Google Maps is on screen or your phone is locked. This keeps the customer's live map, the restaurant and Flavrly support up to date.

- **Android**: a notification stays in your status bar: *Flavrly is sharing your location for an active delivery.*
- **iPhone**: a blue location indicator shows in the status bar.
- Your position is sent about every 15 seconds or 30 metres. If you lose signal, points are saved on the phone and sent in order when you're back online.

## Allowing background location

The first time you accept a delivery, Flavrly shows a screen explaining what it collects and why. Tap **Continue**, then choose **Allow all the time** (Android) or **Always** (iPhone).

If you tap **Not now** or deny it, tracking still works while Flavrly is open, but pauses whenever the app is not on screen. The delivery screen then shows **Tracking pauses in the background** with a button to turn it on or to open your phone settings.

## How it stops

Background tracking stops by itself when:

- the delivery is delivered, cancelled, or closed by support;
- you go offline on **Home**;
- you sign out.

You can also change Flavrly's location permission to **While using the app** in your phone settings. Tracking then only runs while the app is open.

## Good to know

- With the app open and you online, your location is also shared so Flavrly can offer you nearby orders.
- After this update, the background tracking needs the latest app version from the store.
- What is recorded while you're online (location, online/offline times, device, network and battery info) and how long it is kept: see [What Flavrly records while you're online](help:rider-location-and-device-info).
