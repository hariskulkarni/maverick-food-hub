## Open your delivery

On **Home**, tap the card under **CURRENT DELIVERY**. The delivery screen shows the order code, the item count and total, what you earn, a map, and the steps **Reach restaurant**, **Pick up order**, **Reach customer** and **Hand over**.

## The steps

Tap the big button at the bottom of the screen at each stage:

1. **Accept this delivery**: appears only if the order was assigned to you directly. Home shows these as **New request**. Orders you claim from the pool skip this step.
2. **I've reached the restaurant**: tap when you arrive for pickup. Home shows **Heading to pickup** until then.
3. **I've picked up the order**: tap once you have the food. The order goes out for delivery, and Home shows **Out for delivery**.
4. **I've reached the customer**: tap when you're at the drop location.
5. **Confirm delivery**: enter the customer's delivery code and confirm. See [Delivery code and proof photo](help:rider-delivery-code-and-photo).

When it works, you'll see **Delivered!**. Tap **Back to home**.

## Helpful tools on the screen

- **Navigate to pickup** / **Navigate to customer** opens Google Maps directions to your next stop.
- Distance and an approximate time to your next stop, based on your live location.
- **DROP** details: address, floor/flat, landmark, delivery note and the customer's name. Tap the phone icon to call the customer.
- **CUSTOMER NOTE**, if the customer left one.
- On cash orders, a **Collect via UPI / Card** option appears at the customer step. See [Collect cash on delivery](help:rider-cash-on-delivery).

## Good to know

- Keep location access on. If it's off, the app warns you that your position won't appear on the customer's tracker.
- If a step fails, you'll see **Could not update**. Check your connection and tap again.
- Can't reach the customer at the drop? Call them first, then tap **Customer unreachable** under the delivery code box to alert Flavrly support. See [When you can't reach the customer](help:rider-customer-unreachable).
- For other problems, contact support from **Profile > Messages** or raise an **Order issue** ticket in **Help & Support**.
