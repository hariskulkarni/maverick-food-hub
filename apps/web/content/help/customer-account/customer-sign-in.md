## Do I need an account to order?

You can browse restaurants and menus freely, but you need to sign in to place an order. That lets us keep you updated and lets you track your delivery. If you go to checkout while signed out, you are sent to sign in and brought back to checkout afterwards.

## How to sign in

1. Go to [Sign in](/login).
2. Enter your **Mobile number** with country code (for example +91…).
3. Tap **Send OTP** and enter the **6-digit code** you receive.
4. Tap **Verify & sign in**.

## Your profile

Open **Profile** to see your wallet and loyalty balance, recent orders and saved addresses. The profile menu includes:

- **My orders** — your past and current orders.
- **Addresses** — see [Manage your addresses](help:customer-manage-addresses).
- **Favorites**, **Rewards**, **My reviews**, **Signup Bonus** and **Referrals**.
- **Security & sessions** — your recent logins and active devices.

## Security & sessions

Under **Profile > Security & sessions** you can see where you are signed in, **Terminate** a single session, or tap **Sign out everywhere else**. If you suspect someone else has used your account, sign out other sessions and contact support.

## Good to know

- You must be at least 18 to create an account and place orders.
- There is currently no screen to change your name or mobile number yourself. If they need correcting, contact support from the [contact page](/contact).
