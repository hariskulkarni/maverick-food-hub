## List your restaurant

1. Go to [Open your restaurant](/signup/restaurant).
2. Fill in the application to set up your storefront.
3. Our team reviews your application. Kitchens are usually approved in about a day.

Commission and fee terms are agreed with the platform during onboarding — ask our team if you have questions before you apply.

## Become a rider

1. Go to [Become a rider](/signup/rider).
2. Enter your name and mobile number, choose your vehicle (Bike, Scooter, Bicycle or Car) and add your vehicle number. You can also note your availability or preferred zones.
3. Tap **Apply**. Once the team approves you, you can see ready orders from restaurants in a shared pool and pick the ones that fit your route.

Riders are paid per delivery, and customer tips go to the rider who delivered the order. Peak-hour and rain bonuses may be added on top. Learn more about the rider app on the [rider app page](/rider-app).

## I am a partner — how do I sign in?

Approved restaurants sign in from the restaurant login, reached via **Sign in here** on the [Open your restaurant](/signup/restaurant) page. Your dashboard is where you manage your menu, orders and payouts.

## Good to know

For partner and rider questions, see the [contact page](/contact) and pick **I run a restaurant** or **I want to ride**, or send a message from the contact form with the topic **Restaurant partnership** or **Riding with us** – it opens a support ticket and the team replies by email.
