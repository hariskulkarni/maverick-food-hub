## Delete your account

You can delete your account yourself:

1. Go to **Profile > Security & sessions**.
2. Under **Delete my account & data**, tap **Delete my account**.
3. Type **DELETE** to confirm, then tap **Permanently delete**.

This permanently removes your personal data — name, email, phone, saved addresses and photo — and signs you out on every device. Past orders are kept in anonymised form for legal and tax records. This cannot be undone.

> Restaurant staff and partner accounts can't be deleted this way — contact support to close them.

## What data do we collect?

Only what we need to run the service:

- **Account data** — your name, mobile number and email.
- **Order data** — items, delivery addresses, order notes and order history.
- **Location data** — your delivery address and, if you allow it, your device location for live tracking.
- **Payment data** — payment status and transaction references, not your full card details.
- **Device and usage data** — used for security and to improve the service.
- **Communications** — messages to support, ratings and reviews.

We do not sell your personal data. Restaurants and riders receive only what they need to prepare and deliver your order.

## Your rights

You can ask for a summary of your data, correction or erasure, withdraw consent, and raise a grievance. Our [Privacy Policy](/privacy) explains exactly what we collect, how we use it, how long we keep it and how to contact our Grievance Officer.
