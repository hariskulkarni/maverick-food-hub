## What it is

Saved addresses make checkout faster. Your default address is listed first when you choose where to deliver.

## Add an address

1. Go to **Profile > Addresses** (or tap **Add one** / **+ Add another address** at checkout).
2. Choose a label — **Home**, **Work** or **Other**. For **Other**, give the address a name, for example "Mom's place".
3. Fill in the address line, city and PIN code (all required), plus any extra details.
4. Tick **Set as default** if you want this to be your main address.
5. Save the address.

## Edit, delete or change your default

From **Profile > Addresses** you can:

- Open **Edit address** to update an existing address.
- Set a different address as your **Default**.
- Delete an address you no longer use — you will be asked to confirm.

## Good to know

- Whether a restaurant delivers to an address, and the delivery fee, is checked at checkout — see [Delivery area, fees and timing](help:customer-delivery-area-and-fees).
- An accurate address and a reachable phone number help your rider find you. If an order can't be delivered because the address was wrong, a refund may not apply.
- Deleting your account removes all saved addresses.
