## What it is

**Service recovery** (sidebar, under Reviews) lists customers who rated you low, so you can reach out and make it right.

## When a ticket opens

A ticket opens when a customer rates an order at or below your threshold (default 3 stars, set under **Settings > Reviews > Review requests**). This works for ratings from review-request links, the order page and the review QR kit. The overall rating is used, or the lower of food and delivery if there is no overall rating.

There is one ticket per order. If the customer rates again, the ticket is updated.

Restaurant alert contacts get an email and SMS about new tickets, at most once every 30 minutes.

## Work a ticket

Each ticket shows the rating, the order code (or **QR feedback**), the comment, the items, and timing: when the order was placed and delivered, and whether it was on time. Then:

1. Tap **Call** or **WhatsApp** to reach the customer (if they shared a phone number).
2. Set the **Status** – **Open**, **Contacted** or **Resolved** – and an **Assignee**.
3. Add **Notes (internal)** and tap **Save notes**.
4. If you offer something, enter it under **Goodwill gesture (unconditional)** and tap **Record**.

Use the **Open**, **Contacted**, **Resolved** and **All** filters to find tickets.

## The rules

Any refund, replacement or goodwill gesture must be unconditional. **Never ask a customer to change, remove or write a review in exchange for it.** These customers saw the same Google review link as everyone else.

## Good to know

- Service recovery has no reply deadline – but reaching out quickly is what turns a bad experience around.
- Refunds to customers are handled by Flavrly support. Record what you agreed, then contact support if a refund is needed.
