## Review insights

On **Reviews**, tap **Insights**. The page covers the last 90 days of Google reviews:

- **Average rating** (with your overall Google rating), **Reviews**, **Reply rate** (with median reply time) and **Overdue replies**, with a link to reply to them.
- **Weekly trend** and **Rating distribution**.
- **Dishes guests mention** – your own menu dishes, with how often mentions were positive.
- **Recurring issues** – for example food arrived cold, late delivery, packaging or spills, portion size, price or value, and staff behaviour.
- **Team names mentioned** – a passive trend of names guests mention. It is not a performance metric: never use it for quotas, rankings or to ask staff to collect reviews.

The page updates automatically; tap **Refresh** for the latest. With an AI reply model, topics are extracted by AI; without one, by keyword matching.

## Weekly digest

In **Settings > Reviews > Weekly digest**, switch it on to get an email every Monday at 9:00 AM covering the last 7 days: average rating, new reviews, reply rate and time, top praised dishes, top issues and overdue replies. It goes to the owner, restaurant admins and the restaurant contact email.

Turn on **Short version by SMS** or **Short version on WhatsApp** for a text summary too. Tap **Save digest**, and **Send test digest now** to see one straight away.

## Good to know

- Insights use Google reviews. Ratings customers leave in Flavrly are under **Feedback** – see [Customer feedback](help:restaurant-customer-feedback).
