## What it is

**Admin > Reviews** brings your Google reviews into Flavrly so you can read them and reply with AI-assisted drafts. It needs a Google connection first – see [Connect Google reviews](help:restaurant-google-reviews-setup).

## Sync and filter

- Reviews also sync by themselves every 15 minutes when Google Business Profile is connected. Tap **Sync from Google** to pull everything now. If Google is not connected, a link to **Open settings** appears.
- Use **Search reviewer or text…**, filter by **Rating**, and by reply state: **All replies**, **Pending approval**, **Saved drafts**, **Posted**, **Failed**, **Skipped** or **Overdue** (no reply after your reply target).
- Tap **Insights** for trends – see [Review insights and weekly digest](help:restaurant-review-insights).

## Draft inbox

If auto-reply is set up, reviews that need your approval appear in the **Draft inbox** with a **Suggested reply - edit before posting**. Edit the text if needed, then tap **Approve & Post**, or tap **Regenerate** for a new draft. Tap **Save draft** to come back to it later.

- **Insert template…** drops one of your [reply templates](help:restaurant-review-reply-rules) into the reply.
- **Find matching order** shows the order the review most likely refers to (delivered in the 72 hours before the review). It is private context for you and the AI draft and is never posted.
- If you opened the page from an alert, the review appears under **From your alert**.

## Replying from All reviews

How you reply depends on how Google is connected:

- **Google Business Profile connected** – type in **Write a reply…** and tap **Post reply**, or tap **Generate draft** first. Posted replies show "Posted" with the date.
- **Quick connect (Google Places)** – replies can't be posted from Flavrly. Tap **AI draft** (or **Regenerate AI draft**), then **Copy**, and tap **Reply on Google** to paste it there.

## Good to know

- AI drafts are suggestions. Read every draft before posting – it goes out under your restaurant's name.
- Every reply, typed or AI-drafted, passes a safety filter. If it offers something in exchange for a review, asks the guest to change their review, or contains a phone number, email or order details, you'll see **This reply cannot be posted yet** with what to remove, and the post button stays disabled.
- **Failed** means posting (or drafting) didn't work – for example a connection problem. Tap **Retry**.
- After posting, a reply may show **Awaiting Google moderation**. If Google turns it down, you'll see **Google rejected this reply** with the reason – tap **Redraft** for a new draft that takes the reason into account.
- In-app ratings from your own customers are separate – see [Customer feedback](help:restaurant-customer-feedback).
