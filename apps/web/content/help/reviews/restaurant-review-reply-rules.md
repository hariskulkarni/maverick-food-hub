## Reply rules

In **Settings > Reviews > Reply rules & brand voice**, list words under **Hold for approval when a review mentions**. A review containing any of them is never auto-posted, even with a high rating – its draft waits in your Draft inbox. Defaults include food poisoning, allergy, refund, legal, sick and hospital. Tap **Save rules**.

How auto-reply treats each new review:

- **At or above your auto-post rating** – the reply is posted automatically, unless a hold word appears or the draft fails the safety filter.
- **At or below your draft rating** – a draft waits for your approval. These are never posted automatically.
- **In between** – no automatic reply. The review shows as **Skipped** with the reason; reply manually if you like.

## Brand voice

Flavrly learns your tone from your last 20 posted replies (at least 3 are needed) and refreshes it daily. AI drafts follow it, plus any **Tone instructions** in the Auto-reply card. Tap **Re-learn from my replies** after you've posted a few new ones.

## Reply templates

In **Reply templates**, save replies you use often. Use `{name}` for the guest's first name and `{dish}` for a dish they mentioned. No templates yet? Tap **Add starter templates**. In the reviews inbox, pick one from **Insert template…** and edit before posting.

## The safety filter

Every reply – AI-drafted or typed – is checked before it can be posted. You'll see **This reply cannot be posted yet** if it:

- offers discounts, freebies or anything else in exchange for a review,
- asks the guest to change or remove their review,
- contains a phone number or email address,
- includes order details such as an order number or rider name.

Fix the text and the post button becomes available again. Invite guests to contact you privately instead of sharing details in public.

## Good to know

- Auto-reply needs a Google Business Profile connection and an AI reply model. See [Connect Google reviews](help:restaurant-google-reviews-setup).
- Replies can be up to 4,000 characters.
