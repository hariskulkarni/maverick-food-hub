## What it is

After an order is delivered, the customer is asked for feedback. They can rate the food and the overall experience, pick issue tags and leave a comment. Customers can submit and edit feedback within 48 hours. You manage it all in **Admin > Feedback** (Customer feedback).

## What you see

- **Avg Food**, **Avg Overall**, **Total Feedback** and **Low-Rated %** (highlighted when 20% or more).
- Issue tag counts: Missing item, Wrong item, Cold food, Packaging issue, Food quality, Late delivery and Rider behaviour.
- A table with the order, food and overall ratings, tags, comment, time and actions. Sort by clicking **Food**, **Overall** or **When**.

## Filters

- **All** or **Low-rated only (≤2)**.
- **Range**: 7d, 30d or 90d.
- **Status**: All, Published, Hidden or Flagged.

## Actions

- **Hide** removes feedback from public view; **Unhide** publishes it again.
- **Flag** marks feedback for attention – you must enter a reason.
- **Reply** lets you write a public reply to the customer.
- Tick several rows to **Hide** or **Unhide** them in bulk.

Only published feedback appears on public pages.

## Auto-publish setting

In **Settings > Reviews**, **Auto-publish customer reviews** decides whether new feedback goes live immediately or stays hidden until you publish it. See [Connect Google reviews](help:restaurant-google-reviews-setup).

## Good to know

- You can also see an order's feedback on its order detail page (open the order from **Admin > Orders**).
- Moderation actions and replies are recorded in your audit log.
- Google reviews are managed separately in **Admin > Reviews**. See [Google reviews](help:restaurant-google-reviews).
- Low ratings (at or below the threshold you set under **Review requests**, 3 stars by default) open a ticket in **Service recovery** so you can follow up. See [Follow up on low ratings](help:restaurant-service-recovery).
