## What it is

The **Review QR kit** gives you a printable card for tables or delivery bags. Scanning it opens a page where guests rate their visit and, if they like, review you on Google.

Open it from **Settings > Reviews > Review requests > Print review QR kit**, or the **Review QR kit** button on **Service recovery**.

## Print or download

1. Choose **A5 table tent** or **A6 packaging insert**.
2. Tap **Print**, or **Download PNG** to send the file to a print shop.

The card shows your restaurant name, "How was your meal?", the QR code and the link. The page also shows how many times your code has been scanned.

## What happens when a guest scans

The guest sees a short rating page ("How was your visit?") with stars, an optional comment, and optional name and phone if they'd like a call back. The **Review us on Google** button is shown whatever rating they give. A low rating opens a [service-recovery ticket](help:restaurant-service-recovery).

Your QR code doesn't change, so printed cards keep working.

## Good to know

- Don't add offers, discounts or freebies for reviews to the card, and don't ask guests to review while staff watch.
- Scans appear as **QR scans** in the Review requests results.
