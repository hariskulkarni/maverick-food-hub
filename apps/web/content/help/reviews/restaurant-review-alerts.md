## Alerts for low ratings

In **Settings > Reviews > Alerts & reply target** (on by default):

1. Set **Alert on new reviews rated at or below** (default 2 stars).
2. Email goes to the owner, restaurant admins and the restaurant contact email. Turn on **Also send SMS** or **Also send WhatsApp** if you want texts too – they use your SMS / WhatsApp setup.

Each new low review is alerted once, within minutes of Flavrly picking it up. Several reviews found at the same time arrive in one message. The alert includes the AI draft (if an AI reply model is set up) and a link straight to the review.

## Reply target

Set **Reply target (hours)** (default 24). Reviews without a reply after that time show as **Overdue** in the inbox – use the **Overdue** filter on **Reviews**.

## Escalation

Add **Escalate overdue to (email)** or **or phone (SMS)**, for example the owner or an area manager. Each overdue review is escalated once to that contact. Without an escalation contact, nothing is escalated.

## Good to know

- Alerts and escalation cover Google Business Profile reviews. Escalation looks at reviews from the last 14 days created after you first saved these settings.
- Overdue counts also appear in [Review insights](help:restaurant-review-insights) and the weekly digest.
