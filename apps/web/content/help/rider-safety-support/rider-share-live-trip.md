## What it is

**Share my live trip** creates a private link that lets someone you trust follow your location while you ride. It's useful on late-night shifts or long trips.

## How to share

1. Open the **Safety Centre**: **Home > Safety** (Quick actions) or **Profile > Safety Centre**.
2. Under **QUICK ACTIONS**, tap **Share my live trip**.
3. Your phone's share sheet opens with the message *Follow my live delivery trip on Flavrly* and the link. Pick WhatsApp, SMS or any other app and send it.

If the link can't be created, you'll see **Could not create share link**. Check your connection and try again.

## What the other person sees

The link opens a **Live trip** page in any browser – no app or sign-in needed. It shows:

- your **first name** and your trip status (for example *Rider on shift*, or the delivery stage when the link is tied to a delivery),
- your **last known location** on a map, with how long ago it was updated and an **Open in Maps** button,
- the time the link stops working.

The page refreshes by itself. It never shows the customer's name, phone number, address or the order code.

## When the link stops working

- **After 4 hours.** The page then says *This trip link has expired.*
- **When you share a new link.** Only one link is live at a time, so creating a new one switches the old one off.
- **When the delivery ends**, if the link is tied to a delivery. The page then says *This trip has ended. Live location is no longer shared.*

## Good to know

- The location shown is the last one your phone sent. If location access is off, the page says no location has been received yet.
- Sharing your trip doesn't alert anyone. In an emergency, call 112 and use [SOS](help:rider-sos-emergency).
