## First: call 112

In a life-threatening emergency, **call 112 first**. Then use SOS so Flavrly's team knows too.

## What SOS does

SOS creates an emergency alert from your account. It includes your GPS location (if you've allowed location access). Two things happen:

- **Flavrly's safety and support team** sees the alert straight away.
- **Your emergency contacts get a text message.** Every contact saved under **Emergency Contacts** is texted (up to 5 numbers), with your name, the time of the alert and a map link to your location. If your location wasn't available, the message says so.

Texting your contacts needs SMS to be set up on Flavrly's side. If it isn't, or if you haven't saved any contacts, the safety team still gets your alert.

## How to trigger SOS

1. Open the **Safety Centre**: **Home > Safety** (Quick actions) or **Profile > Safety Centre**.
2. Tap the red **SOS — Emergency** button.
3. Tap **Trigger SOS** to confirm.

Your phone vibrates, and the button changes to a pulsing **SOS ACTIVE** panel: *Help has been alerted with your location.* Stay where you are if it's safe.

If sending fails, you'll see **Could not send SOS**. Try again, or call 112 directly.

## When you're safe

On the **SOS ACTIVE** panel, tap **Mark safe / resolve**, then **I'm safe**. The alert closes.

If the support team closes the alert for you, the app picks that up within about 15 seconds.

## Good to know

- You can only have one active SOS at a time. Tapping SOS again while one is active doesn't create a second alert.
- If location access is off, the alert is still sent, just without your position.
- Your contacts get a text message, not a call. If you can, call them yourself as well. Keep their numbers up to date in [Manage your emergency contacts](help:rider-emergency-contacts).
- Tapping SOS again while an alert is active doesn't text your contacts a second time.
- For something that isn't an emergency, such as an accident report after the fact or a breakdown, use [Report an incident](help:rider-report-incident).
