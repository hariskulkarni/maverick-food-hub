## What it is

Emergency contacts are people you trust, such as family or friends, saved on your rider account. One of them can be marked **PRIMARY**.

Open **Safety Centre > Emergency Contacts**. To get to the Safety Centre, go to **Home > Safety** or **Profile > Safety Centre**.

## Add a contact

1. Under **ADD A CONTACT**, enter a **Name** and a **Phone number**. Both are required.
2. Optionally, add a **Relationship** (for example, Spouse, Parent or Friend).
3. Turn on **Set as primary contact** if this should be your main contact.
4. Tap **Add contact**.

## Edit or remove a contact

- Tap the **pencil** next to a contact, change the details, then tap **Save changes** (or **Cancel**).
- Tap the **bin** icon, then **Remove**, to delete a contact.

## The primary contact

Only one contact can be primary. If you make a new contact primary, the old primary loses that label automatically. The primary contact is listed first.

## Good to know

- The screen says your primary contact is alerted during an SOS, but the current app doesn't send them a message or call itself. An SOS goes to Flavrly's safety and support team. If you need your contact, call them yourself. See [Use SOS in an emergency](help:rider-sos-emergency).
- Keep the numbers up to date so the support team can reach the right person if they need to.
