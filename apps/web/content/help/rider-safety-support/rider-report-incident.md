## What it is

Use **Report an Incident** to tell Flavrly's safety team about anything that affected your safety or a delivery. In an emergency, call 112 and use [SOS](help:rider-sos-emergency) instead.

Open **Safety Centre > Report an Incident**. To get to the Safety Centre, go to **Home > Safety** or **Profile > Safety Centre**.

## How to file a report

1. Under **WHAT HAPPENED?**, pick a type: **Accident**, **Harassment**, **Vehicle breakdown**, **Theft**, **Unsafe location**, **Customer dispute** or **Something else**.
2. Under **DESCRIBE IT**, write what happened, where, and who was involved. Use at least 10 characters and up to 1,000.
3. Tap **Submit report**, then confirm.

You'll see **Report submitted**. Your current location is attached automatically if you've allowed location access.

## Add a photo

Under **PHOTO (OPTIONAL)**, tap **Add a photo**, then **Take photo** or **Choose from library**. Tap the photo again to **Change or remove** it.

When you submit, the photo is uploaded first and attached to your report so the safety team can see it. If the upload fails, you'll see **Photo upload failed** with two choices:

- **Submit without photo** – sends the report straight away, without the picture.
- **Cancel** – keeps the form open so you can try again when your connection is better.

If you haven't allowed camera or photo access, the app tells you how to enable it in Settings. You can still submit without a photo.

## Follow your reports

Your reports are listed under **MY PAST REPORTS** with a status:

- **Open**: received.
- **Under review**: the safety team is looking into it.
- **Resolved** / **Closed**: finished.

If the safety team adds a note, it appears under **SAFETY TEAM** on that report.

## Good to know

For problems that aren't about safety, such as payments, the app or your account, raise a support ticket instead. See [Raise and follow a support ticket](help:rider-support-tickets).
