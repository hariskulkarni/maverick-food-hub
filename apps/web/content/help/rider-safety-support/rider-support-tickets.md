## What it is

**Help & Support** is where you raise a ticket with Flavrly's support team and follow the replies. Open it from **Profile > Help & Support**.

## Raise a ticket

1. Tap **Raise a ticket**.
2. Enter a short **Subject**.
3. Pick a **Category**: **Payment**, **App bug**, **Order issue**, **KYC**, **Account**, **Safety** or **Other**.
4. Under **Describe your issue**, give the details: the order code, what happened, and when.
5. Tap **Submit ticket**.

You need both a subject and a description to submit.

## Follow your tickets

Your tickets are listed under **YOUR TICKETS**. Each one shows the latest message (*You:* or *Support:*), when it was last updated, and a status:

- **Open**: waiting for support.
- **In progress**: support is working on it.
- **Needs your reply**: support is waiting for you.
- **Resolved**: support thinks it's sorted.
- **Closed**: finished.

## Reply to support

Tap a ticket to open the conversation. Type in **Type your reply…** and tap the arrow to send.

- Replying to a **Resolved** ticket reopens it as **Open**.
- You can't reply to a **Closed** ticket. Raise a new one if you still need help.

## Good to know

- The screen also has a **FREQUENTLY ASKED** section. If an FAQ answer doesn't match what you see in the app, trust the app and these help articles, and ask support to confirm.
- To chat with your restaurant or platform support, there's also **Profile > Messages**.
- Use [Report an incident](help:rider-report-incident) for safety incidents, and [SOS](help:rider-sos-emergency) in an emergency.
