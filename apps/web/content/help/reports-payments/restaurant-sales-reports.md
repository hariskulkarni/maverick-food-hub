## What it is

**Admin > Reports** gives you charts for the last 30 days and downloadable reports for any date range.

## On-screen insights

- **Sales (30 days)** chart.
- **Best sellers** (top 10) and **Least sellers** (bottom 5).
- **Customer insights** – customers, repeat rate, average order value (AOV) and orders per customer.
- **Rider performance (30d)** – deliveries done and failed, average minutes and rating for each rider.

## Download a report

1. Pick a range: **Today**, **7 days**, **30 days**, **90 days** or **Custom** (choose **From** and **To**).
2. Find the report you need and tap **Download CSV** or **Download XLSX**.

Available reports:

- **Daily sales** – per-day orders, net sales, tax, delivery fee and gross totals.
- **Item sales** – each item's quantity sold and revenue.
- **Cancelled orders** – order code, customer, reason and refunded amount.
- **Payment mode** – count and total for each payment method.
- **Taxes** – daily tax collected.
- **Delivery fees** – daily delivery fee revenue and order counts.

## Group restaurants

If your restaurant heads a group and group report sharing is enabled, the sales chart is rolled up across the group and a **By restaurant (30 days)** table appears. If sharing is off, a note explains that you're seeing only this restaurant's numbers.

## Good to know

- **Daily sales** leaves out cancelled orders and orders whose payment failed.
- Reports are based on when the order was placed.
- On-screen insights use your first active branch.
- For payment methods and settlement, see [Payments overview](help:restaurant-payments-overview).
