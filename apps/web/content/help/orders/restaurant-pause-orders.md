## What it is

The pause control at the top of **Admin > Orders** lets you stop taking new orders when the kitchen is overloaded, something has run out, or you need to close early.

It shows the current state:

- **Open — accepting orders** (green), or
- **Paused** (red), with the time left or "until you resume", and the reason.

## How to pause

1. Tap **Pause orders**.
2. Pick a **Reason**: Too busy, Out of stock, Equipment issue, Closing early or Other.
3. Choose how long: **15 minutes**, **30 minutes**, **1 hour** or **Until I resume**.

A confirmation appears, for example "Branch paused for 30 min".

## How to resume

Tap **Resume**. You will see "Branch is taking orders again". Timed pauses also end on their own when the time runs out.

## What pausing does

- Pausing switches the branch's **Accepting orders** setting off. New orders at that branch are refused with "Branch is not accepting new orders right now" until you resume.
- Only one pause is active at a time; starting a new pause replaces the old one.
- Orders already placed are not affected – keep preparing and dispatching them as normal.
- Your dashboard, menu, combos, reports, menu import and coupons keep working while the branch is paused. A paused branch never triggers the **No branch yet** notice – that notice only appears when the restaurant has no branch at all.

## Good to know

- The pause applies to your primary branch (the one the Orders page is showing).
- If only a few dishes are unavailable, hide those items instead of pausing everything. See [Item availability](help:menu-item-availability).
- You can also switch **Accepting orders** on or off in **Settings > Branches**. See [Branch settings](help:restaurant-branch-settings).
