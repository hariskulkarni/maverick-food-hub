## What it is

A KOT (kitchen order ticket) is a short printout that tells the kitchen what to cook. Flavrly builds it as a simple page sized for narrow receipt paper (about 300 pixels wide) and opens your browser's print dialog automatically.

## How to print a KOT

Tap **KOT** on any of these:

- an order card on the [kitchen board](help:kitchen-board),
- an order card on **Admin > Orders**,
- the order detail page (open an order from the Orders board).

The ticket opens in a new tab and the print dialog appears. Choose your kitchen printer and print.

## What's on the ticket

- The heading **KOT**, your branch name, the order code and the date and time the order was placed.
- One line per item with the quantity and item name, plus any notes the customer added to that item.
- The customer's order notes, if any.
- An "end of ticket" line.

## Printing an invoice

On **Admin > Orders** and on the order detail page, the **Invoice** (or **Invoice PDF**) button opens a PDF invoice for the order, which you can print or save.

## Good to know

- Printing goes through your browser, so the printer must be set up on the device you are using. Flavrly does not send tickets to printers automatically.
- You can reprint a KOT any time – it is generated fresh from the order each time.
- Combo breakdowns are shown on the kitchen board card; the KOT prints the item line as ordered.
