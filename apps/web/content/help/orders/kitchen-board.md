## What it is

The kitchen board at [/kitchen](/kitchen) is a live screen for the cooking line. Kitchen and admin accounts can open it. It shows every open order in four columns:

- **New** – orders waiting to be accepted (Received).
- **Accepted** – confirmed, not yet started.
- **Preparing** – being cooked.
- **Ready** – packed and waiting for pickup.

Orders appear and move by themselves; you don't need to reload.

## How to work an order

1. In **New**, tap **Accept order**.
2. In **Accepted**, tap **Start preparing**.
3. In **Preparing**, tap **Mark ready**.

Each card shows the items and quantities, the order code, the time since it started, and a pickup code for self-pickup orders. Combo items list what goes into the combo, so you know exactly what to plate. Tap **KOT** to print a kitchen ticket – see [KOT printing](help:kot-printing).

## New order alarm

When an order arrives, a banner says "New order — needs attention" and a chime loops every few seconds. Tap **ACKNOWLEDGE** to stop it. Use the **Sound on / Sound off** button to mute the chime on this device.

## Late orders

A card gets a red outline when a **New** order has waited more than 5 minutes since it was placed, or an **Accepted** or **Preparing** order is more than 25 minutes past acceptance. The timer on each card shows the minutes elapsed.

## Good to know

- If the account covers several restaurants, filter tabs and coloured labels show which restaurant each order belongs to.
- The **Live** badge means real-time sync is working. If it shows "Synced Ns ago", tap **Refresh**.
- Admins can do the same steps from **Admin > Orders**; both screens stay in sync.
