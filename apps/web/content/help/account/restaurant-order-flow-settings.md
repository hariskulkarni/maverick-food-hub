## What it is

**Settings > Order flow** controls how orders are accepted and which order types customers can choose. Open **Settings**, select the **Order flow** tab, and tap the card to open the settings page.

## The settings

- **Auto-accept orders** – new orders are accepted automatically without manual confirmation. Cash and wallet orders are accepted as soon as they are placed; online orders once their payment completes.
- **Allow scheduled orders** – customers can place orders for a later time slot.
- **Allow self-pickup** – customers can collect their order from the counter instead of having it delivered. Pickup orders show a pickup code on the Orders and kitchen boards.
- **Dine-in reservations** – customers can reserve a table. When this is on, set the **Deposit (₹)**, **Discount (%)** and **Default slot (min)**.
- **Freebies / gifts** – reward customers with a free gift item when their order clears a spend threshold. The rules themselves are set under **Storefront CMS > Promotions > Freebies**.

Tap **Save changes**. You will see "Order-flow settings saved".

## Good to know

- If auto-accept fails for any reason, the order simply waits in the **New** column for a manual accept – it is never lost.
- With auto-accept on, the kitchen still needs to tap **Start preparing** and **Mark ready**.
- Pickup and dine-in orders don't use riders.
- For reservations, add your tables in **Admin > Tables** (name, capacity, sort order) and manage bookings in **Admin > Reservations**.
- Only admins can change these settings.
