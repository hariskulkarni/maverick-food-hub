## What it is

Your storefront is your public restaurant page at /r/your-restaurant-slug. **Storefront CMS** (in the sidebar under Storefront) controls how it looks. Its tabs are **Design**, **Menu**, **Promotions** and **Integrations**.

## Design your page

On the **Design** tab you can edit:

- **Hero & Carousel** – the big banner at the top.
- **Branding** and **Theme — colours & fonts**.
- **Announcement bar** – a slim promo bar pinned to the top.
- **About / Story section** and **Content blocks** (text, images, galleries, calls-to-action, videos or maps, spacers).
- **Social & contact links**, **SEO & sharing** and a **Footer note**.
- **Info bar (under the hero)** – open status, delivery time, rating, city and verified badge.
- **Layout & Sections**, **Top sellers section** and **Combos section**.

Tap **Save storefront design** when you're done, and use **Preview live storefront** to check the result.

## Menu order on the storefront

Under **Menu categories — order & visibility**, reorder categories and show or hide them, then tap **Save menu order**. To edit the dishes themselves, use the **Menu** tab. See [Categories and schedules](help:menu-categories).

## QR codes

- Your restaurant's storefront QR codes are created by the platform team when your restaurant is set up. There is no QR generator in the restaurant admin.
- Scanning one of these codes opens your storefront, and each scan is counted.
- A code stops working if it is switched off or your restaurant is not active.
- For promo posters, a [coupon campaign](help:restaurant-coupon-campaigns) gives you a printable QR poster that opens your storefront with the code already applied.

## Good to know

- Basic details customers see, such as your logo, cover image and tagline, can also be edited in **Settings > Branding** (Storefront branding).
- If you manage several restaurants, a picker at the top of Storefront CMS lets you choose which one you're editing.
- Contact platform support if you need new or replacement QR codes, for example for tables.
