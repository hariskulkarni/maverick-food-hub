## What it is

Restaurant staff use one of two roles. Each account is linked to your restaurant, so staff only see your restaurant's orders and data.

## Admin

- Full access to the restaurant admin at [/admin](/admin): dashboard, orders, menu, promotions, delivery zones, riders, reports, reviews, storefront and settings.
- Can also open the kitchen board at [/kitchen](/kitchen).
- Only admins can manage rider assignment and change order-flow settings.
- Signs in at [/login?mode=admin](/login?mode=admin&next=/admin).

## Kitchen

- Uses the [kitchen board](help:kitchen-board) at [/kitchen](/kitchen) to accept orders, move them to Preparing and Ready, and print KOTs.
- Cannot open the admin pages – trying to sends them back to the sign-in page.
- A kitchen login attached to a parent restaurant covers the kitchens of its child restaurants on one consolidated board.

## How staff accounts are created

- The **owner account** is created when you sign up your restaurant and has the Admin role. See [Sign up and get approved](help:restaurant-signup-and-approval).
- Additional Admin and Kitchen accounts are created by the platform team when they set up your restaurant. Each new account gets a temporary password, which the platform team shares with you.
- There is currently no page in the restaurant admin to add or remove staff yourself. Contact platform support to add a team member, change a role or remove access.

## Good to know

- Give the Kitchen role to anyone who only needs to work the cooking line – they can't see reports, payments or settings.
- If your account manages several restaurants, use the restaurant switcher at the top of the admin sidebar to change which one you're working on.
- Each person should have their own login, so the order timeline records who changed each order.
