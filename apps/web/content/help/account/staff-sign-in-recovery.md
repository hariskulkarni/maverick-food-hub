## Too many attempts

After 5 wrong passwords in a short time, sign-in is paused for that email. The sign-in page tells you how many minutes to wait. You can also ask a platform super admin to **Unlock sign-in** for you.

## You were given a temporary password

When an account is created for you, or a platform super admin resets your password, you sign in once with that temporary password and are asked to **choose a new password** (at least 12 characters) before anything else. Then you set up or enter your Google Authenticator code as usual.

## You lost your Google Authenticator

- **Use a recovery code.** When you first set up Google Authenticator you were shown 10 recovery codes. On the code step, tap **Lost your authenticator? Use a recovery code** and type one of them. Each code works once.
- **No codes left?** Ask a platform super admin to **Reset authenticator** for you. At your next sign-in you scan a fresh QR code, and get new recovery codes.

Resetting your password by email does **not** remove your authenticator — that is on purpose, so someone who gets into your mailbox still can't take over your account.

## Account suspended or closed

If your password is correct but the account is suspended or closed, the sign-in page says so. Only a platform super admin can reinstate it.
