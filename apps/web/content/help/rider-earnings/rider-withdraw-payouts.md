## What it is

The **Instant Payout** screen is where you request a withdrawal of the money you've earned. Open it from **Earnings > PAYMENTS > Instant Payout**.

## Your balance

- **AVAILABLE TO WITHDRAW**: your lifetime earnings minus every withdrawal that's already been paid or is still in progress.
- **Lifetime**: everything you've earned, including incentive bonuses.
- **Paid out**: the total of withdrawals marked paid.

## How to request a withdrawal

1. Under **WITHDRAW NOW**, type an **Amount**, or tap **MAX** to fill in your full balance.
2. Under **Pay to**, choose **UPI** and enter your **UPI ID**, or choose **Bank account** to use the bank account on file with Flavrly.
3. Tap **Withdraw instantly**, then confirm.

You can't withdraw more than your available balance. If you have nothing to withdraw, the button reads **Nothing to withdraw yet**.

## What happens next

Your request is recorded with the status **REQUESTED**, and the amount comes off your available balance straight away. The Flavrly team then sends the money and marks the withdrawal **PAID**. Follow it under **PAYOUT HISTORY**:

- **REQUESTED** / **PROCESSING**: not paid yet.
- **PAID**: the transfer is done. A reference may appear as *Ref …*.
- **FAILED**: the payout didn't go through. A failed amount is no longer subtracted from your available balance, so you can withdraw it again.

> The app may say the money settles instantly and show a "Paid!" message as soon as you submit. The status in **PAYOUT HISTORY** is what tells you whether you've actually been paid. Pull down to refresh it.

## Good to know

- The app doesn't promise a payout date. If a withdrawal stays unpaid, raise a **Payment** ticket in **Help & Support**. See [Raise and follow a support ticket](help:rider-support-tickets).
- COD cash you're holding is separate from your balance. It's money you owe for deposit, not money you can withdraw.
