## What it is

The **Payouts** screen is where you ask for the money you've earned to be sent to you. Open it from **Earnings > PAYMENTS > Payouts**.

Payouts aren't instant. Each request is processed by the Flavrly team, who make the transfer and update its status.

## Your balance

- **AVAILABLE TO WITHDRAW**: your lifetime earnings minus every withdrawal that's already been paid or is still in progress.
- **Lifetime**: everything you've earned, including incentive bonuses.
- **Paid out**: the total of withdrawals marked paid.

## How to request a withdrawal

1. Under **REQUEST A WITHDRAWAL**, type an **Amount**, or tap **MAX** to fill in your full balance.
2. Under **Pay to**, choose **UPI** and enter your **UPI ID**, or choose **Bank account** to use the bank account on file with Flavrly.
3. Tap **Request withdrawal**, then **Request** to confirm.

You'll see **Withdrawal requested** with the amount and its status.

You can't withdraw more than your available balance. If you have nothing to withdraw, the button reads **Nothing to withdraw yet**.

## What happens next

Your request is recorded and the amount comes off your available balance straight away. Follow it under **PAYOUT HISTORY**, where each request shows one of these statuses:

- **Requested**: received, waiting for the Flavrly team.
- **Processing**: the team is making the transfer.
- **Paid**: the money has been sent. A reference may appear as *Ref …*.
- **Failed**: the payout didn't go through. The amount goes back into your available balance, so you can request it again.

Pull down on the screen to refresh the statuses.

## Good to know

- The app doesn't promise a payout date. If a request stays at **Requested** or **Processing** for longer than you expect, raise a **Payment** ticket in **Help & Support**. See [Raise and follow a support ticket](help:rider-support-tickets).
- COD cash you're holding is separate from your balance. It's money you owe for deposit, not money you can withdraw.
