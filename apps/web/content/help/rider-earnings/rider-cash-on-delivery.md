## What it is

On a cash-on-delivery (COD) order, you collect the order total from the customer at the door. Cash you collect isn't part of your earnings. You hold it until you deposit it.

## If the customer wants to pay by UPI or card

On a COD order, when you reach the customer step, the card **Customer wants to pay digitally?** appears.

1. Tap **Collect via UPI / Card**.
2. Read the message (*"Don't take cash as well."*) and tap **Open payment page**.
3. The payment page opens inside the app. Let the customer pay there by UPI or card.
4. Close the page. The app shows **Confirming payment…** while it checks with the payment provider.
5. When it's confirmed, you'll see **Paid digitally — ₹amount. Don't collect any cash for this order.**

The amount is always the order total. You can't change it. If payment isn't confirmed within about 4 minutes, you'll see **Still not confirmed. Collect cash instead, or try again.** Tap **Try digital payment again**, or take cash.

Either way, you still finish with the customer's delivery code. See [Delivery code and proof photo](help:rider-delivery-code-and-photo).

## Track your cash: Cash in Hand

Open **Earnings > PAYMENTS > Cash in Hand**.

- **CASH TO DEPOSIT**: cash you've collected (in full or in part) that hasn't been reconciled yet. Hand it over at your branch.
- COD orders are grouped as **Still to collect**, **Cash in hand — deposit soon**, **Needs attention** and **Settled**.
- Each row shows the order code, the time, the amount and a status: **To collect**, **Collected**, **Partial**, **Deposit pending**, **Mismatch**, **Reconciled** or **Waived**. If you collected a different amount than expected, the row also shows *Expected ₹…*.

## Good to know

- An order paid digitally shows as **Collected** with ₹0 cash, plus a note that it was paid digitally. It doesn't add to your cash to deposit.
- The Flavrly team updates the statuses when they check your collections and deposits. If something shows **Mismatch**, raise a **Payment** ticket in **Help & Support**.
