## Incentives

Open **Earnings > Incentives** or **Home > Incentives**.

- **BONUS UP FOR GRABS** adds up the bonuses you haven't won yet.
- Each card under **ACTIVE INCENTIVES** shows a delivery target, your progress, and the bonus.
- Targets are either **daily** (deliveries since the start of today) or **weekly** (deliveries since Monday).

When you confirm the delivery that reaches a target, the bonus is added to your lifetime earnings automatically. It's paid once per period and counts toward your withdrawable balance. If nothing is running, you'll see **No incentives running right now**.

## Surge pay

When surge is live, a banner on **Home** and **Earnings** reads, for example, **1.5× pay live near [zone]**.

- Surge applies when the order's **delivery address** is inside a live surge zone at the moment you claim it.
- It raises the core delivery pay (base, distance and time) by the multiplier, as a surge bonus.
- It's included in the amount fixed when you claim, so you'll see it in what you earn for that order. The total still has to fall within the minimum and maximum pay per delivery.

## Your tier

Open **Profile > My Tier**. Your tier depends on your lifetime deliveries **and** your rating:

- **BRONZE**: everyone starts here.
- **SILVER**: 50+ deliveries and a 4.0+ rating.
- **GOLD**: 200+ deliveries and a 4.3+ rating.
- **PLATINUM**: 500+ deliveries and a 4.6+ rating.

The screen shows a progress ring toward the next tier, your deliveries and rating, and a list of every tier.

Higher tiers keep more of the surge bonus: **5%** more at Silver, **12%** more at Gold and **20%** more at Platinum. This applies only to orders with surge.

## Good to know

- Your rating comes from customer delivery ratings. See [See your customer ratings and feedback](help:rider-customer-ratings).
- To withdraw bonuses, see [Request a payout of your earnings](help:rider-withdraw-payouts).
