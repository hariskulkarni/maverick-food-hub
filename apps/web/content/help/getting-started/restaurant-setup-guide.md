## What it is

The **Setup guide** (sidebar, rocket icon, or [/admin/onboarding](/admin/onboarding)) is a 10-step wizard that gets a new restaurant ready to take orders. Only a restaurant admin (owner or manager) can save changes in it.

The first time an admin opens the dashboard of a restaurant with no orders yet, Flavrly opens the setup guide for you once. After that, use the sidebar link or the **Get your restaurant live** card on the dashboard.

## The ten steps

1. **Business profile & branding** – name, contact, logo and colours, with a live storefront preview. *Required.*
2. **Legal & compliance** – the FSSAI licence for each branch (14-digit number, **Licence type** and **Valid until**), plus optional GSTIN, PAN and legal name. *Required.*
3. **Branch & opening hours** – address, map pin (paste coordinates or a Maps link and tap **Use these**) and weekly hours. Presets such as **11:00–23:00 daily** and **Copy to all days** save typing. *Required.*
4. **Menu** – **Start from a template** (North Indian, South Indian, Chinese, Café / Bakery or Pizza – editable samples), **Import a spreadsheet**, or **Add dishes one by one**. You need at least one available dish to continue. *Required.*
5. **Delivery zones & fees** – pick a preset such as **3 km, flat ₹30** and tap **Create this zone**. Every branch needs a delivery area to continue. *Required.*
6. **Staff & roles** – add **Kitchen staff** or a **Manager (admin)**. The login is shown only once, so tap **Copy login details**. *Can be skipped.*
7. **Payments** – review your commission, online payment fee and settlement cycle. These are set by your agreement with Flavrly; contact platform support to change them. *Can be skipped.*
8. **Notification channels** – SMS, WhatsApp and email for order updates. *Can be skipped.*
9. **Storefront & QR** – preview your page and **Generate my QR code**. *Can be skipped.*
10. **Readiness check & go live** – final checks, then **Go live**. See [Go-live checks](help:restaurant-go-live-checks). *Required.*

## Moving through the guide

- Use **Next** and **Back**, or tap any step in the stepper at the top.
- **Skip for now** appears on optional steps. A skipped step stays on your checklist. Required steps can't be skipped.
- Your current step and half-filled forms are saved as you go. Leave any time with **Back to dashboard** and continue later.
- In the stepper, green means done, dashed means skipped, and amber means a step is marked done but one of its required checks is failing.

## The dashboard checklist

The **Get your restaurant live** card on the dashboard shows your progress, any failing required checks and unfinished steps. Tap **Start setup** or **Continue setup** to jump back in. After you go live it shrinks to **Setup complete**, and you can **Dismiss** it. If a required check starts failing later, the card tells you what changed.

## Good to know

- You can revisit any step after going live to make changes.
- Menu templates are samples at typical prices – untick what you don't serve and change names and prices before you go live.
