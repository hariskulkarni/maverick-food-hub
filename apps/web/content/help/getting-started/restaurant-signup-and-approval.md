## What it is

New restaurants apply through the partner sign-up form at [/signup/restaurant](/signup/restaurant). Submitting the form creates your **owner account** and a restaurant in **Pending** status. The platform team reviews it before you can take orders.

## How to apply

The form has five steps:

1. **Account** – your name, email and a password of at least 8 characters. You will sign in with this email after approval.
2. **Identity** – restaurant name, cuisine, tagline, description, contact email and phone, plus a logo and cover image.
3. **Location** – branch name, address, city, PIN code, optional latitude and longitude, and your operating hours for each day.
4. **First menu** – add at least one signature dish (name, price, veg or non-veg, photo). You can add the full menu after approval.
5. **Submit** – review everything and tap **Submit for approval**.

If the email is already used by another account, the form is rejected and you need a different email.

## After you submit

Sign in at [/login?mode=admin](/login?mode=admin&next=/admin). Until your restaurant is approved, the admin area shows a status screen instead of the dashboard:

- **Pending** – awaiting platform approval.
- **Suspended** – temporarily suspended.
- **Rejected** – the reason given by the platform team is shown on screen.

Once the status is **Active**, you get the full admin dashboard and your restaurant can accept orders.

## Good to know

- If you see **No restaurant linked**, your account is not attached to any restaurant yet.
- Everything you entered (branding, address, hours, menu) can be edited later from the admin area. See [Branch settings](help:restaurant-branch-settings) and [Add and edit menu items](help:menu-items-manage).
- If a status screen looks wrong, contact platform support.
