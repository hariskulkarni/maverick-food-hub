## What it is

Each branch is one physical location with its own menu, riders and orders. Branch details decide where you deliver from, when you take orders and how charges are calculated.

## How to edit a branch

1. Go to **Settings** and open the **Branches** tab.
2. Expand the branch you want to change.
3. Update the fields and tap **Save branch**.

You can change:

- **Branch name**, **Phone**, **Address**, **City**, **State / region**, **Postal code** and **Country**.
- The map pin, or **Latitude** and **Longitude** as a fallback.
- **Delivery radius (km)**, **Tax rate (%)**, **Base delivery fee (₹)**, **Per-km delivery fee (₹)** and **Packaging fee (₹)**.
- **Operating hours** for each day. A closed day shows "No orders accepted on this day."
- Food licence details: **Licence number**, **Licence type** (Registration, State or Central), licensee name, premises address, issue, renewal and expiry dates, and a photo of the certificate.
- The **Accepting orders** switch.

## Adding a branch

Open **Admin > Branches** (My branches) and tap **+ Add branch**. Enter the name, address, city, PIN, contact details, tax % and delivery fees, then tap **Create branch**. The My branches page lists each branch with its menu item, order and rider counts.

## Good to know

- If a branch has active delivery zones, the zone's fee rules are used instead of the base and per-km fee. See [Delivery zones](help:restaurant-delivery-zones).
- A banner appears at the top of Settings when a branch licence needs attention.
- Pausing orders from the Orders page turns **Accepting orders** off until you resume. See [Pause incoming orders](help:restaurant-pause-orders).
- The menu, dashboard and reports pages work on your first active branch.
