## What it is

**Readiness check & go live** is step 10 of the [setup guide](help:restaurant-setup-guide). It lists **Required** and **Recommended** checks, each with a count of how many pass.

## Required checks

**Go live** stays disabled until all of these pass. Only active branches are checked.

- **Restaurant name and contact phone** – a name and a valid Indian phone number.
- **At least one active branch with address and map pin** – address, city, a valid PIN code and a map pin for every active branch.
- **Valid FSSAI licence for every branch** – a 14-digit number that hasn't expired.
- **Opening hours set** – saved hours with at least one open day for every branch.
- **At least one dish available to order** – priced above ₹0, marked available, in an active category.
- **Delivery area set** – every branch has an active delivery zone, or a map pin and a service radius.

## Recommended checks

These don't block go-live, but customers trust a complete page more:

- **Logo uploaded**
- **GSTIN added**
- **Kitchen or manager login added**
- **Storefront QR code generated**

## Fix and re-check

Each failing check has a **Fix in …** button (for example **Fix in Legal**) that opens the right step. After fixing, tap **Re-check**.

## Go live

Tap **Go live**. Flavrly runs the checks again, marks your setup complete and creates your storefront QR code if you don't have one yet.

- **Already approved by Flavrly:** you'll see **You are live! 🎉** and *You are live and taking orders.* Keep the Orders screen open – new orders ring there.
- **Not approved yet:** you'll see **Setup complete — waiting for Flavrly approval.** Customers see your restaurant once the Flavrly team approves it. See [Sign up and approval](help:restaurant-signup-and-approval).

Use **Open Orders** or **View my page** to carry on.

## Good to know

- Pressing **Go live** doesn't make your restaurant visible on its own – approval by the Flavrly team does.
- If something changes later (for example an FSSAI licence expires), the dashboard checklist tells you which required items need attention.
