## Where to find it

- **Admins:** **Training** in the sidebar ([/admin/training](/admin/training)).
- **Kitchen staff:** the **Training** link at the top of the kitchen board ([/kitchen/training](/kitchen/training)).

Modules are written and published by Flavrly. Kitchen staff see kitchen modules (for example **FSSAI hygiene basics**, **Allergen handling** and **Using the Flavrly kitchen board**). Admins see admin modules (such as **Managing your menu** and **Handling reviews and complaints**) and can also take the kitchen modules as optional extras.

## Your library

The **Library** tab lists modules under **Required** and **Optional**, with the time each takes and a status: **Not started**, **In progress**, **Quiz pending**, **Certified**, **Re-certify** or **Overdue**. Required modules have a due date (usually 14 days); after that they show as **Overdue**. If some modules have a Hindi version, switch with **English** / **हिन्दी**.

## Take a module

1. Open a module and work through the lessons (text, pictures, videos or checklists). Tap **Mark done and continue** on each.
2. When every lesson is done, the quiz unlocks. Answer every question and tap **Submit answers**.
3. Score at least the pass mark (80% for Flavrly's modules) to pass. You'll see **You passed!** and your certificate is issued.

If you don't pass, review the lessons and tap **Retake quiz**. Required modules have a short wait (5 minutes) before you can retry. Correct answers are shown once you pass.

## Certificates

Certificates appear in the **Certificates** tab with a code (for example *FLV-…*), the module version, date and score. A certificate stays valid until Flavrly publishes a new version of the module. Then the module shows **Re-certify** – redo the lessons and quiz to stay current. Your old certificate stays on record.

## Track your team (admins)

On the admin **Training** page, **Your team** lists each staff member with their role, **Required done** and **Status** (**Complete**, **In progress** or the number overdue). Tap **Export CSV** to download it.

## Good to know

- Training doesn't block any work on the dashboard or kitchen board; overdue modules are just flagged.
- Restaurants can't add their own modules. Ask Flavrly support if you need a topic covered or quiz attempts reset.
