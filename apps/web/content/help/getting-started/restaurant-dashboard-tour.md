## What it is

**Admin > Dashboard** is the first page you see after signing in. It gives a live snapshot of today's business for your restaurant.

## What the dashboard shows

- **Orders today**, **Revenue today**, **Pending** (shows "Action required" when orders are waiting) and **Out for delivery**.
- **Revenue, last 14 days** chart and **Payment mix (today)**.
- **Best sellers** and **Peak hours**.
- **Customers** – customer count, repeat rate, average order value and average orders per customer.
- **Recent menu changes** – the latest menu and integration changes, with **View all** opening the Activity page.
- **Latest orders** – the six newest orders; **All orders** opens the Orders board.

If your restaurant heads a group and group report sharing is on, the headline numbers cover every restaurant in the group and a **By restaurant (today)** table appears.

## Finding your way around

The sidebar holds the day-to-day pages: **Dashboard**, **Setup guide**, **Orders**, **Reservations**, **Tables**, **Live tracking**, **Branches**, **Delivery Zones**, **Dedicated Riders**, **Rider Safety**, **Messages**, **Reports**, **Activity**, **Feedback**, **Reviews**, **Service recovery** and **Training**. **Help center** is further down the sidebar.

Under the **Storefront** heading you will find:

- **Storefront CMS** – tabs for **Design**, **Menu**, **Promotions** (offers, coupons, coupon campaigns, happy hours, freebies, challenges, cross-sell, combos) and **Integrations**.
- **Settings** – branding, order flow, branches, integrations, reviews and notifications.

If your account manages more than one restaurant, the restaurant name at the top becomes a switcher.

## Good to know

- Most dashboard cards use your primary branch – the oldest active branch, or your oldest branch if all are paused. Pausing orders doesn't hide a branch. Only a restaurant with no branch at all sees the **No branch yet** notice, with links to **Manage branches** and **Open settings**.
- While your restaurant is new, a **Get your restaurant live** card links to the [setup guide](help:restaurant-setup-guide).
- For guides on any page, open the [help center](help:restaurant-help-center).
- See [Accept and manage orders](help:restaurant-accept-orders) to start working with orders.
