## Where to find it

- **Admins:** **Help center** in the sidebar (life-buoy icon), or [/admin/help](/admin/help).
- **Kitchen staff:** the **Help** link in the top bar of the kitchen screen ([/kitchen/help](/kitchen/help)). Kitchen accounts see the kitchen guides.

## Search

Type at least two letters in **Search help articles…**. Results appear as you type, with matching words highlighted. Search copes with small typos. If nothing matches, try different words or browse the topics below the search box.

## Help for the page you're on

On **Orders**, **Menu**, **Reports**, **Reviews**, **Dedicated Riders** and **Settings**, a round **?** button opens **Help for this page** – the guides for what you can do there. Read an article right in the dialog, or tap **Browse all help topics**.

## Rate an article

At the end of each article, answer **Was this article helpful?** with **Yes** or **No**. You can add an optional comment and tap **Send comment**. Your feedback goes to the Flavrly team that writes these guides.

## Contact support

If an article doesn't answer your question, use **Still need help?** > **Contact support** on the help center or any article. Describe what you need, add an **Order ID** if it's about an order, and tap **Send**. This opens a ticket with the Flavrly support team, with the article you were reading attached, and they will get back to you.

## Good to know

- Some articles have a Hindi version. Tap **हिंदी में पढ़ें** (or **Read in English**) on the article.
- Customers have their own help pages at [/faq](/faq).
