## What it is

Every order has its own page showing its status, a timeline, the items and the total. Once a rider is on the way you can follow them on a live map.

## How to open the tracker

- Right after checkout you are taken to your order.
- Open **Profile > My orders** (or [/orders](/orders)) and pick an order.
- Or go to [Track an order](/track), enter your order code (for example **ORD-AB12CD**) and tap **Track**.

You need to be signed in to the account that placed the order to see its details.

## What you will see

- **Status steps:** Order Received, Accepted, Preparing, Ready for Pickup, Out for Delivery, Delivered.
- **ETA:** an estimated time that updates as the order moves through these steps. When the rider's location is available, an "Arriving in ~N min" pill shows their distance from you. These are estimates, not guarantees — they depend on the kitchen, distance, traffic and weather.
- **Live map:** the restaurant, your delivery address and your rider.
- **Support** and **WhatsApp** buttons to contact the support team about this order.

## Delivery OTP

Your order page shows a delivery OTP. The rider asks for it at handover and enters it to close the order. Read it out only once your food is in your hands, and never share it before the rider is at your door.

## Good to know

- Please stay reachable on your contact number while the order is on its way.
- After delivery you can **Share feedback** within 48 hours, and use **Order again** to rebuild the same cart.
