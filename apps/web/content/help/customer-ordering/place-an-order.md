## What it is

You can order straight from your browser — no app download is needed. Browse restaurants near you, open a kitchen, add dishes to your cart and check out. You can also open a restaurant directly by scanning its QR code.

## How to place an order

1. Open a restaurant and add dishes to your cart.
2. Go to **Checkout**. If you are not signed in yet, you will be asked to sign in with your mobile number first — your cart and restaurant are kept for when you come back. See [Sign in and your account](help:customer-sign-in).
3. Under **How would you like your order?** choose **Delivery** (to your door) or **Pickup** (collect yourself), where the restaurant offers it.
4. For delivery, pick an address under **Deliver to**, or tap **Add one** to save a new address.
5. Under **When?** choose **Order now** or, if the restaurant allows it, **Schedule for later** and pick a date and time.
6. Optionally enter a **Coupon**, switch on your **Wallet** or **Loyalty** balance, and add **Order notes**.
7. Choose a payment method and place the order. The **Order summary** shows the subtotal, discounts, tax, packaging and delivery fee before you pay.

## Good to know

- Each order is placed with a single kitchen so it can be cooked and delivered as one batch. To order from two restaurants, place two separate orders.
- Pickup orders have no delivery fee. You get a pickup code to show at the counter when the order is ready.
- Your order is confirmed once the restaurant accepts it.
- After ordering, you can follow it from your order page — see [Track your order](help:track-your-order).
