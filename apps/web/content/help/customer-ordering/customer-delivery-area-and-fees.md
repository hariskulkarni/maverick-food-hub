## Do you deliver to my area?

We are expanding city by city, and each restaurant delivers to its own area. Choose your delivery address at checkout to see whether that kitchen currently delivers to you. If it doesn't, checkout shows a message such as "this address is outside our delivery area" or "we do not deliver to this address right now", and you won't be able to place a delivery order to that address. You can still choose **Pickup** if the restaurant offers it.

## Delivery fee and minimum order

- The delivery fee is calculated for your address and shown in the **Order summary** before you pay, sometimes with the approximate distance in km.
- Some areas have a minimum order value. If your cart is below it, checkout shows the minimum (for example "minimum order for this area is ₹…") and you will need to add more items.
- Pickup orders have no delivery fee.

## How long will it take?

Delivery time depends on the kitchen, distance, traffic and weather. Your order page shows an estimated time that updates as your order progresses — it is an estimate, not a guarantee. See [Track your order](help:track-your-order).

## What if no one is home?

Please be reachable on the contact number on your account and have your delivery OTP ready. If the rider cannot reach you or deliver after reasonable attempts, the order may be marked as not delivered. A refund may not apply when an order couldn't be delivered because the address was wrong or you were unreachable — see our [Refund & Cancellation Policy](/refunds).
