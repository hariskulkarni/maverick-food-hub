## What it is

Coupons are codes customers type at checkout for a discount. Find them under **Storefront CMS > Promotions > Coupons**. The table shows each coupon's code, type, minimum order, limits, validity, usage and an **Active** switch.

## Quick start

If you have no coupons yet, tap **Create WELCOME50**. It creates a coupon worth ₹50 off with a ₹250 minimum order, which you can edit afterwards.

## Create a coupon

1. Tap **New coupon**.
2. Enter a **Code**. It is converted to capitals; only letters, numbers, hyphens and underscores are kept.
3. Pick the **Branch** and add a **Description** – this is what customers see.
4. Choose **Flat amount** (enter **Flat off (₹)**) or **Percentage** (enter **Percent off (%)** and a **Cap (max ₹ off)**).
5. Set **Min order (₹)**, **Per-user limit**, **Total usage limit**, **Valid from** and **Valid until**.
6. Tap **Create coupon**.

To edit, click the coupon's row. To stop a coupon, turn off its **Active** switch or open it and tap **Deactivate** – existing redemptions stay intact.

## What checkout checks

When a customer enters a coupon code, it is applied only if:

- the coupon is active and its **Valid until** date has not passed,
- the total usage limit has not been reached,
- the customer has not already used it up to the per-user limit, and
- the cart subtotal meets the minimum order.

The discount is capped so it never exceeds the cart subtotal.

## Good to know

- Coupons marked **Expired** in the list are past their end date.
- For automatic discounts, first-order deals, buy-one-get-one and scheduled promotions, use [Offers](help:restaurant-offers) instead – offers have their own eligibility rules and can apply without a code.
- To print codes on receipts or posters and track redemptions, use [Coupon campaigns](help:restaurant-coupon-campaigns).
