## What it is

**Admin > Live tracking** shows a live map of riders who are working an order for your restaurant. Click any pin for ETA, trail and contact information.

## What's on the map

- **Riders** with an active job for your orders – assigned, accepted or picked up. A rider only appears once their location is known.
- **Your branch** pins (branches with a map location set).
- **Customer drop points** for those orders, when the delivery address has coordinates.

Before pickup, a rider is shown heading to your branch. After pickup, they are shown heading to the customer.

## How to use it

1. Open **Live tracking** from the sidebar.
2. Click a rider pin to see their ETA, recent trail and contact details.
3. Use this alongside **Admin > Orders**, which shows each order's status and the rider who picked it up.

## Group restaurants

If your restaurant heads a group, the map covers riders working orders for any restaurant in the group, and branch pins are labelled with the restaurant name.

## Good to know

- Riders appear only while they have an active job for you. Delivered orders drop off the map.
- Set your branch's map pin in **Settings > Branches** so your pickup point appears. See [Branch settings](help:restaurant-branch-settings).
- Branches that are paused or switched off are not shown on the map.
- To control which riders can take your orders, see [Dedicated riders](help:restaurant-dedicated-riders).
