## Open training

Go to **Profile > Training & Certification**, or tap the training banner on **Home** if you have modules pending.

The **Training** screen shows how many modules you hold a certificate for and lists them under **REQUIRED** and **OPTIONAL**, each with a status: **Not started**, **In progress**, **Quiz pending**, **Certified**, **Re-certify** or **Overdue**. Required modules are due within about 7 days. Switch between **English** and **हिन्दी** where a Hindi version exists.

Required modules include **Road safety and your helmet**, **Food hygiene in transit**, **Customer handover etiquette**, **Cash on delivery and payments** and **Using the Flavrly rider app**.

## Take a module

1. Open a module and read each lesson. Video lessons open with **Watch the video**.
2. Tap **Mark done and continue** on each lesson, then **Mark done and take the quiz**.
3. Answer the questions. Pass with at least 80% and you'll see **You passed! Certificate issued.**

If you don't pass, tap **Review lessons and retake**. Required modules have a short wait (5 minutes) before you can retry.

## Certificates

Tap the ribbon icon on the Training screen to open **Certificates**. Each shows the date, version, score and certificate code. When Flavrly updates a module, it shows **Re-certify** – redo the lessons and quiz to stay current.

## Going online

Flavrly can require safety training before you go online. If that is switched on and you haven't passed a required safety module (such as **Road safety and your helmet**), going online is refused with **Training required**. Tap **Open training**, finish the module, then go online again. Going offline is never blocked.

## Offline

Modules you've opened while online are saved on your phone, so you can keep reading without signal (*Offline copy saved …*). Marking lessons done and submitting a quiz need a connection.

## Good to know

If you've used all your quiz attempts on a module, contact Flavrly support from **Help & Support** to reset them.
