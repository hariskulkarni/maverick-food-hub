## Open the help center

Go to **Profile > Help & Support**, then tap the **Help center** card under **HELP ARTICLES**.

## Search

Type at least two letters in **Search help…**. If nothing matches, try other words or raise a support ticket.

## Hindi or English

Tap **हिं** / **EN** at the top right of the help center to switch language. On an article, tap **हिंदी में पढ़ें** or **Read in English** when the other version exists. If an article has no Hindi version, you see the English one.

## Reading offline

The article list and every article you open are saved on your phone:

- With no connection, the list shows *You are offline — showing help saved on …*, and search looks only through saved articles.
- An article you've opened before shows *Offline — saved copy from …*.
- An article you haven't opened yet can't be shown offline. Tap **Try again** when you're back online.

Open the articles you need most (for example SOS) once while online, so they're ready when your signal is weak.

## Rate an article

At the end of an article, answer **Was this article helpful?** with **Yes** or **No**, and add an optional comment. Rating isn't available while you're reading the offline copy.

## Still stuck?

Tap **Still stuck? Raise a support ticket** (or **Still need help? Raise a ticket** on an article). See [Raise and follow a support ticket](help:rider-support-tickets). In an emergency, call 112 and use [SOS](help:rider-sos-emergency).
