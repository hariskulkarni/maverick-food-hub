## What it is

KYC documents prove who you are and that your vehicle is road-legal. Flavrly keeps one document of each type for you:

- Aadhaar
- Driving Licence
- Vehicle Insurance
- Vehicle RC
- PAN Card

## How to check your documents

1. Open the **Profile** tab.
2. Scroll to **MY DOCUMENTS**.
3. Each document shows its type, the last 4 digits of the document number, and a status badge.

Tap a document with an eye icon to open it full-screen. That way you can show it to traffic police or building security without leaving the app.

## What the statuses mean

- **PENDING**: submitted and waiting for review.
- **APPROVED**: verified.
- **REJECTED**: not accepted. Submit a corrected document.
- **EXPIRED**: the document is past its validity. Submit a current one.

## Good to know

- You can't upload or replace documents in the rider app. The app only shows their status. If **MY DOCUMENTS** says *No KYC documents on file*, or a document is rejected or expired, raise a ticket with the **KYC** category from **Profile > Help & Support**. See [Raise and follow a support ticket](help:rider-support-tickets).
- The app shows the status but not the reason a document was rejected. Ask in your KYC ticket.
- PAN and driving licence numbers may be checked automatically against official records after they're submitted. Insurance and RC documents are reviewed by the Flavrly team.
- When a document is replaced, it goes back to **PENDING** until it's reviewed again.
- Driving licence, insurance and RC documents need an expiry date in the future.
