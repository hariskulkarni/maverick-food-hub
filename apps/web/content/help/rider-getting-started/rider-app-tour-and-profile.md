## The five tabs

- **Home**: your online/offline switch, the delivery you're on now, a break option and quick actions (Demand Map, Safety, Shifts, Incentives).
- **Orders**: ready orders you can claim.
- **Activity**: one list of your recent runs, newest first, including deliveries in progress and completed ones.
- **Earnings**: today's earnings, goals, payouts, cash in hand and incentives.
- **Profile**: your details, documents, and links to every other rider tool.

## The welcome tour

The first time you open the app, a short tour of four cards appears: go online, claim a delivery from the pool, follow the step-by-step flow, and track your earnings. Tap **Next** to move through it, or **Skip** to close it. After you tap **Get started** or **Skip**, it won't show again on that phone.

## Your Profile

At the top of **Profile** you see your photo, name, phone number and your rider type:

- **Fleet rider**: you see orders from restaurants across the platform.
- **Dedicated rider · restaurant name**: you're attached to one restaurant, and the Orders tab shows only orders from that restaurant (or its group).

Your tier badge also appears here once it loads.

### Change your photo or name

- Tap your photo, then choose **Take photo** or **Choose from library**. Allow camera or photo access if your phone asks.
- Tap **Edit name**, type your name, then tap **Save**.

### Rider tools on Profile

- **MESSAGES**: Messages, to chat with your restaurant and platform support.
- **SAFETY & WORK**: Safety Centre, My Shifts, Delivery Preferences.
- **GROWTH & SUPPORT**: My Tier, Customer Feedback, Refer & Earn, Training & Certification, Help & Support.

**Training & Certification** has short learning modules with quizzes and certificates – see [Rider training and certificates](help:rider-training). **Help & Support** opens the [help center](help:rider-help-center) and your support tickets.

## Good to know

Pull down on Home, Orders or Profile to refresh. To learn about documents, see [Check your KYC documents](help:rider-kyc-documents).
