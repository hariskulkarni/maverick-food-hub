## What to do straight away

If your phone is lost or stolen, contact your Flavrly operations team (or raise a support ticket from another phone) and ask them to **sign you out everywhere**.

A platform super admin does this from **Platform → Users**: they open your profile and tap **Sign out everywhere**. Every device that was signed in to your rider account is signed out on its next request, so nobody can use your account from the lost phone.

## Signing in on your new phone

1. Install the Flavrly rider app on the new phone.
2. Sign in with your **registered rider phone number** and the 6-digit code — see [Sign in to the rider app with your phone](help:rider-sign-in-with-otp).
3. If your SIM was lost too, get it replaced by your mobile operator first: codes are only sent to your registered number.

## Changing your phone number

If you now use a different number, your operations team must update the number on your rider account before you can sign in with it.

## Good to know

- Your earnings, shifts and documents stay on your account; they are not stored on the phone.
- Signing in on a new phone signs out the old one automatically — only one device stays signed in.
