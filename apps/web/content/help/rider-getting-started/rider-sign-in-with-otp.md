## What it is

The Flavrly rider app has no password. You sign in with the phone number you registered as a rider, and a one-time verification code is sent to that number.

## How to sign in

1. Open the app. On **Sign in to start delivering**, type your 10-digit mobile number. The **+91** prefix is already filled in.
2. Tap **Send verification code**.
3. On the next screen, enter the **6-digit code** sent to your phone.
4. Tap verify. You land on the **Home** tab.

If the code doesn't arrive, tap **Didn't get it? Resend code**.

## Common messages

- **No code arrives for your number.** Only registered rider numbers are sent a code (for privacy the app doesn't say whether a number is registered). Check the number, use **Resend code** after the timer, and if it still doesn't arrive, apply on the [Become a rider](/signup/rider) page or contact your operations team.
- **"That code is incorrect or has expired."** Check the digits, or ask for a new code with **Resend code**.
- **"This number is not a rider account."** The number belongs to a different kind of Flavrly account, not a rider account.
- If you ask for codes too many times, the app tells you to wait before trying again.

## Staying signed in and signing out

Your session is saved securely on your phone, so you stay signed in when you close and reopen the app.

To sign out, go to **Profile** and tap **Sign out** at the bottom (there's also a **Sign out** link at the bottom of Home). You'll need your phone number and a new code to sign back in.

## Good to know

- Signing in is not the same as being approved. Until the Flavrly team approves your rider profile, you can't see or claim orders.
- The first time you sign in, a short welcome tour appears. See [Find your way around the rider app](help:rider-app-tour-and-profile).
