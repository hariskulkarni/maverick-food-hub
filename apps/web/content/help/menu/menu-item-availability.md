## What it is

Every menu item has an availability switch. When an item is **Hidden**, customers no longer see it on your storefront and it cannot be ordered.

## How to hide or show one item

1. Go to **Storefront CMS > Menu** and open the **Items** tab.
2. Use the switch on the item's row. The label next to it reads **Available** or **Hidden**.

The change saves straight away. If the save fails (for example your session expired), the switch flips back and an error is shown.

## How to change many items at once

Tick the items, then tap **Make available** or **Make hidden** in the bulk action bar.

## Sizes and add-on options

Inside an item's **Variants & add-ons** section, each size and each add-on option has its own **Available** switch. Use it when, say, only the large size has run out.

## Whole sections

To take a whole category off the menu, use the category's **Enabled / Disabled** switch, or **Disable category + all items** from its More actions menu. To limit a category to certain hours, give it a schedule. See [Categories and schedules](help:menu-categories).

## Good to know

- Checkout checks availability again on the server, so a customer cannot order an item that was hidden after they opened your page.
- A combo cannot be ordered while any item inside it is unavailable.
- Hiding is better than deleting for temporary stock-outs: hidden items keep their photo, price, sizes and add-ons.
- If you need to stop all orders for a while (not just some items), pause the branch from the Orders page. See [Pause incoming orders](help:restaurant-pause-orders).
