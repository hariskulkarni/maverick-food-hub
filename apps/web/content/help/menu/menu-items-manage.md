## What it is

The menu editor lives under **Storefront CMS > Menu** (also at [/admin/menu](/admin/menu)). It has an **Items** tab and a **Categories** tab, plus a **Bulk import & export** panel at the top.

## How to add an item

1. Open the **Items** tab and tap **New item**.
2. Fill in the details listed below.
3. Tap **Create item**.

The item dialog has:

- **Photo**, **Name** (required) and **Description** (a good place to mention allergens).
- **Price (₹)** and **Prep time (min)** – between 1 and 180 minutes.
- **Category** and **Spicy level** (Mild or one to three chillies).
- Switches for **Vegetarian**, **Available**, **Popular** and **Recommended**.

To change an item later, tap the pencil (**Edit**) on its row, make changes and tap **Save changes**. Sizes and add-ons are added from the same dialog once the item exists – see [Sizes, variants and add-ons](help:menu-variants-add-ons).

## Bulk actions

Tick the rows you want (or **Select all**), then choose **Make available**, **Make hidden**, **Mark popular**, **Unmark popular**, **Mark recommended**, **Unmark recommended** or **Delete selected**.

## Deleting items

Tap the bin icon on a row, or use **Delete selected**. Deleting cannot be undone. What happens:

- Past orders keep the item's name and price, so invoices stay correct.
- Any combo memberships and freebie rules that use the item are removed.

If you only want to stop selling an item for a while, hide it instead of deleting it. See [Item availability](help:menu-item-availability).

## Good to know

- The menu editor works on your restaurant's first active branch.
- Bulk changes, bulk deletes, category switches and schedule changes are recorded in **Activity** and appear under **Recent menu changes** on the dashboard.
