## What it is

Categories group your items into menu sections. Each category can be switched on or off, and can optionally follow a weekly schedule.

## Add or remove a category

1. Go to **Storefront CMS > Menu** and open the **Categories** tab.
2. Type a name in **New category name** and tap **Add**.
3. To delete, tap the bin icon and confirm. If the category is still in use, deletion fails.

## Enable or disable a category

Use the **Enabled / Disabled** switch on the category row. The **More actions** menu (three dots) also offers **Disable category + all items** and **Enable category + all items**, which switch every item in the category at the same time.

## Schedule a category

1. Tap **Set schedule** on the category row.
2. Turn the schedule on. Pick a day tab (Sun to Sat) and use **Add window** to set start and end times. A day can have more than one window.
3. Use **Copy to all 7 days** or a quick preset: **Breakfast** (07:00–11:00), **Lunch** (12:00–15:00), **Dinner** (18:00–23:00) or **Weekend Specials**.
4. Tap **Save schedule**.

Each window must end after it starts. With the schedule on, you need at least one window, or turn the schedule off.

## Status badges

- **Always on** – no schedule, category enabled.
- **Available now** – inside a scheduled window.
- **Off-hours** – outside every window.
- **No schedule** – schedule turned on but no windows saved.
- **Disabled** – switched off.

## Good to know

- Customers cannot add items from a disabled or off-hours category, and checkout rejects them. Your storefront shows when an off-hours category opens next.
- The kitchen still sees the category; only ordering is blocked.
- To reorder categories or hide them on your storefront, use **Storefront CMS > Design > Menu categories — order & visibility**.
- Schedule changes are recorded in the admin Activity log.
